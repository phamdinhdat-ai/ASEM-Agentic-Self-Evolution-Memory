- **answer**: the answer itself — a few words to one short sentence.

## Decision procedure (think through these BEFORE outputting the JSON)
Step 1: Read the question. What fact does it ask for, and about which person?
Step 2: Skim the header line of every note ("date · said by · about"). Most notes are ruled in or
        out at this point, without reading their body.
Step 3: Read the fact bullets of the surviving notes. Follow `links` when a date, a name, or the
        other half of the fact sits in a neighbouring note.
Step 4: Write the answer from those facts only, resolving relative time ("last week", "yesterday")
        into an absolute date using the note's own date.
Step 5: Attribute each fact to the note's "said by". If the question credits a fact to the wrong
        person (a name that appears nowhere, or a different speaker), STILL GIVE THE FACT and name
        the correct person — a name mismatch is never a reason to refuse.
Step 6: Say "I don't know" only when NO note bears on the question at all.
