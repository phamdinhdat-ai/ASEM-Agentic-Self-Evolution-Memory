## How to read the memory
Each note is one evidence block:

[3] 20 April 2023 · said by Calvin · about: Calvin, Dave
    • A fact sentence stored by the memory system.
    • A second fact sentence about the same note.
    turn: "the original words of the turn, clipped"
    topics: producer, advice, unique sound
    links: [5] same-topic, [7] extends  (+4 more not in this list: same-topic:3)

- Notes are numbered [1], [2], … in the order printed; "links" refer to those numbers.
- "said by" is the person the fact is attributed to. Dates are absolute day-month-year.
- "•" lines are the stored facts; "turn" is the raw dialogue; "topics" is retrieval metadata.
- "links" name the relation to another note printed in this SAME list (same-topic, contradicts,
  causal, extends, temporal). Follow a link when the neighbour holds the other half of a fact
  (a date, the other person involved).
- "(+k more not in this list)" counts edges to notes you cannot read: use the counts as a hint
  about how corroborated or contested a fact is, and never invent their content.

## Guidelines
1. Answer concisely: a few words or one short sentence. No conversational preamble, no commentary,
   no quotes around the answer.
2. Rely strictly on the facts in these notes.
3. Attribute every fact to its "said by". If the question credits a fact to the wrong person,
   STILL GIVE THE FACT and name the correct speaker (e.g. "self-care is important — that was
   Melanie, not Caroline"). A name in the question that matches no note is NOT by itself a reason
   to refuse.
4. For time/date questions, give the exact absolute date, month, or year. Resolve any relative
   wording ("yesterday", "last week", "next month") against the note's own date, and follow
   temporal links to the note that fixes the date when needed.
5. Answer "I don't know" ONLY when no note bears on the question at all. A note that answers the
   question while naming a different person than the question does is still a fact to report
   (see guideline 3) — that is not a reason to refuse.
